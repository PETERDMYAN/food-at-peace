Privacy Policy — Food at Peace

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Food at Peace ("the app", "we") is a calorie and nutrition tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your nutrition and health data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your iPhone only (in the app's private storage). It is not transmitted to us and we cannot see it:

  • Your profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goal — used to calculate your calorie and protein targets.
  • Food entries you log: calories, protein, saturated fat, meal, serving, and time.
  • Your weight log.

You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, the app reads your active and basal energy burned, body weight, and workouts from Apple Health (this includes Garmin and other data that flows into Apple Health) to estimate how many calories you can still eat. If you allow it, the app also writes the calories, protein, and saturated fat you log back to Apple Health.

This data is read from and written to Apple Health on your device. We do not receive it, and it is never transmitted to our servers (we operate none). Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. Your use of Apple Health is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Photo nutrition analysis (Anthropic)

The optional "analyze a photo" feature lets you take or choose a photo of a meal. When you use it, that single photo is sent to the Anthropic (Claude) API to estimate the meal's calories, protein, and saturated fat. The estimate is returned to your device for you to confirm before logging.

  • Photos are sent only when you actively trigger an analysis. The app does not upload your photo library.
  • The photo is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection solely to produce the estimate.
  • Anthropic's handling of API data is governed by the Anthropic Privacy Policy. Anthropic does not train its models on data submitted through its API by default.

The Anthropic API key used for this feature is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device.

4. Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app feedback form, the message you type is submitted to a Google Form so we can read it. Only what you write is sent — providing an email is optional and used only to reply to you. Don't include sensitive information in feedback messages.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. We don't have access to your nutrition or health data in the first place.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
